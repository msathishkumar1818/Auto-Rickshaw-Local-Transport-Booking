Our Journey • Street Mobility Reimagined

Why We Started AutoPulse: Ending The Daily Street Standoff

Every day across Indian cities, millions of commuters exit metro stations and office hubs facing the same dilemma: high app cab cancellations, 2.5x surge pricing, or street bargaining with auto drivers where neither side walks away feeling respected.

We realized that the three-wheeler auto rickshaw is the most agile, space-efficient, and accessible transport vehicle in the world. It didn't need to be replaced by expensive sedans—it just needed an honest digital backbone that respects both the commuter and the captain.

✓ The AutoPulse Guarantee: Zero Commission Model

Unlike corporate taxi aggregators taking 25% to 35% from every ride, AutoPulse charges drivers zero commission on meter fares. Drivers keep 100% of what the meter shows, paid instantly via UPI to their bank account.
