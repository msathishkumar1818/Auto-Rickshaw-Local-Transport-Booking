Tariff Integrity

Understanding The 25% Night Surcharge

Between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM, drivers work through the night to ensure safe connectivity. The law allows an official 25% night charge. Here is how it is transparently computed:

Regular Day Fare (e.g. 6 km): ₹97.50
+ 25% Regulated Night Rate: +₹24.38
Total Legitimate Night Fare: ₹122.00

In street hailing, drivers routinely demand 2x or 3x cash at night. AutoPulse prevents this by enforcing the exact 1.25x statutory rate on digital receipts.
