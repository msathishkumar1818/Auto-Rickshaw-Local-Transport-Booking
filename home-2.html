Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tariffs, booking methods, and night transit safety.

All AutoPulse rides are billed strictly according to the government approved RTO rate card: ₹30 for the first 1.5 km (base fare) and ₹15 per subsequent kilometer. Between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM, a transparent 25% night surcharge applies. There is never any arbitrary surge pricing during rain or peak office hours.
Yes! You can dial our toll-free hotline at 1800-419-AUTO from any basic mobile phone or landline. Our multilingual voice IVR and live dispatch operator will book your auto and send confirmation details via standard SMS.
AutoPulse enforces a strict Zero-Overcharge Guarantee. If a driver asks for cash beyond the digital meter reading, report it with 1 tap in the app, reply to your SMS, or call our 24/7 supervisor desk. We will instantly refund the discrepancy and penalize the driver partner.
Standard small backpacks and hand purses are completely free. For large suitcases or heavy shopping boxes exceeding 15 kg, a government regulated luggage fee of ₹10 per bag applies and is clearly included in your digital receipt.
